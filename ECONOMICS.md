Assumptions
Traffic
Let’s say the website gets around:
10,000 visitors per month
If 10% of them complete the audit, that would mean:
about 1,000 completed audits every month
Lead Capture
Now assume around 20% of users submit their email after seeing the audit results.
That gives:
roughly 200 qualified leads per month
These are pretty strong leads because:
they already use AI tools regularly
they’re already spending money on AI
they’ve already seen that they might be overspending
Consultation Conversion
If even 5% of those leads book a consultation call, that becomes
around 10 calls per month.And if around 20% of those consultation calls convert into paying customers:

That’s:
2 paying customers every month
Example Revenue
If the average customer value is around:
$2,000/year
Then:
2 customers/month = around $4,000 in new monthly recurring revenue added
Which roughly becomes:
~$48,000 ARR
Why This Model Makes Sense
At least initially, the product works more like:
a lead generation engine
a trust-building tool
and a visibility layer for AI spending
instead of trying to be a huge standalone SaaS product from day one.
The audit helps create:
awareness
urgency
and conversation opportunities
for bigger optimization or consulting services later.
The biggest risk is People may want to see where they’re overspending, but they may not want outside help fixing it.
So the audit itself has to feel genuinely useful and trustworthy even without the consulting side.
