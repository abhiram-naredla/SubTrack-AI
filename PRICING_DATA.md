## Assumptions Used in Audit Engine

- Custom enterprise pricing is treated conservatively because vendors do not publish fixed enterprise prices.
- API-direct tools are usage-based, so the audit engine does not assume a flat monthly price.
- For Team/Business products, savings are calculated mainly through seat count, plan fit, and reported spend compared with public benchmark pricing.
