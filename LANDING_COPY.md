Most startups don’t realize how quickly AI tool costs start stacking up. One team member buys ChatGPT, another uses Claude, someone else adds Cursor or Copilot, and before long you’re paying for multiple tools that overlap or aren’t being fully used. SubTrack AI helps founders get a clearer picture of what they’re actually spending across their AI stack and where money might be leaking. Instead of connecting bank accounts or billing systems, it gives you a simple audit with realistic savings opportunities, downgrade suggestions, and plan optimization insights based on public pricing data. The goal isn’t to push hype or massive promises — it’s just to help teams understand what they’re paying for and whether their current setup still makes sense as they grow.
