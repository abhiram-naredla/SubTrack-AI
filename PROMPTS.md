# AI Summary Prompt

System Prompt:
You are an AI infrastructure cost consultant helping startups reduce overspending on AI tools. Keep responses concise, financially grounded, and practical.

Why this prompt:
- Avoids exaggerated savings claims
- Produces founder-focused language
- Keeps summaries concise and screenshot-friendly

What didn’t work:
- Longer prompts produced repetitive summaries
- Aggressive cost-cutting tone reduced trustworthiness
- Purely AI-generated recommendations became financially inconsistent

Important decision:
The audit calculations themselves are deterministic and rule-based. AI is only used for human-readable summarization.
